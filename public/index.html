Before You Start

If you are new to computer-based examinations, read these simple instructions first.

1. Choose your programmeSelect the nursing or midwifery programme you are preparing for.
2. Choose your modeUse Practice mode to learn without a timer or Timed Mock to simulate exam pressure.
3. Select an answerClick one option. You can return to a question and change your answer.
4. Use the navigatorThe question numbers show which questions you have answered and where you are.
5. Watch the timerIn a timed mock, keep an eye on the remaining time and work steadily.
6. Submit carefullyReview your answers before submitting. Your practice result is shown after submission.

CBT Familiarisation

CBT means Computer-Based Test: an examination presented on a computer where candidates use the screen, mouse and keyboard to answer questions.

  1. Read the question carefully.
  2. Click the answer you think is correct.
  3. Use Next and Previous to move around.
  4. Use the navigator to jump to any question.
  5. You may change an answer before submission.
  6. In a timed mock, monitor the countdown.
  7. Use Submit Examination only when you are ready.
